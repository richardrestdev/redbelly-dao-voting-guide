The step-by-step guide to Redbelly DAO voting.

Voting has moved off Snapshot and onto the Redbelly governance platform. Here's how it works now, from getting your wallet verified to seeing your vote land. New to voting? Chapters 01 and 02 are all you need for your first vote.

Last verified 30 Oct 2026

Why can't I vote?Your wallet has to be verified before the proposal's snapshot, taken just before voting opens. When is that checked?Once per proposal, at its snapshot block. How many votes do I get?One per verified address, or your RBNT (Redbelly's own token) at the snapshot. The proposal says which.
01/ 05 · Get verified

Get verified.You only do it once.

Only verified wallets can vote. You verify on Redbelly Access, not on the voting site, and it costs nothing.

1

Connect your wallet

Connect MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet. That address becomes your public address on the network.

access.redbelly.network
2

Verify with Averer

Averer, the accredited issuer, checks you're a real person using your passport.

passport-backed check
3

Enable the wallet

Your wallet gets the Redbelly Network Access Credential (the voting site calls it a Receptor credential). You sign one transaction, and the issuer covers the fees.

one-time transaction

Got more than one wallet? You can link extras to the same identity on Access. Chapter 04 covers how they're counted.

02/ 05 · Cast a vote

Cast a vote.Four steps, no gas.

A proposal is a question the DAO votes on. Open a live one on the governance platform, connect the same wallet, pick an option and sign. Signing proves the vote came from your wallet. Unlike a transaction, it costs no gas, the fee the network charges to process a transaction.

  1. 4Open the proposal and connectOpen a proposal under Active and select Connect wallet. Use a browser wallet, or scan the WalletConnect QR code with your phone. Connect the wallet you verified.
  2. 5Check you're eligible and chooseThe panel should say Eligible to vote and show your voting power. Pick an option. A reason is optional.
  3. 6Review and signSelect Review and sign, then approve the signature in your wallet. No gas.
  4. 7Vote recordedYour vote appears in the Votes list. An IPFS receipt link follows: a public copy of your signed vote that anyone can check. While voting is open you can use Change vote.

The practice panel is modelled on the real one, using a past proposal as the example. It doesn't touch your wallet.

03/ 05 · The snapshot rule

The snapshot rule.Why verifying late doesn't count.

If you're verified and still can't vote, it's usually timing.

Every proposal has a snapshot block. The Redbelly chain records activity in numbered blocks, one after another, so a block number works like a timestamp. The snapshot block is the one recorded when the proposal is published, just before voting opens. It's on the proposal's Details tab. For the Resubmission Policy vote it was 3208192. It has nothing to do with Snapshot, the old voting tool.

Your wallet is judged at that block only: was it verified, and on RBNT-weighted votes, how much did it hold. Verify after a proposal opens and you sit that one out, even with days of voting left. Later proposals will count you. RBNT that arrives mid-vote doesn't count either.

That way every voter is measured at the same moment, and nobody's eligibility shifts while votes come in.

04/ 05 · Counting

How many votes?It depends on the proposal.

The author picks how votes are counted, and it's locked once the proposal is live. The voting system shows under the title. The strategy, which sets your voting power, is on the Details tab.

strategy · verified-identity

One verified address, one vote

Every wallet verified at the snapshot gets one vote, however much RBNT it holds.

1.00voting power per address
strategy · native-balance

RBNT balance

RBNT is the Redbelly Network's own token. Your voting power is the RBNT your wallet held at the snapshot block. The wallet also had to be verified by then.

RBNTheld at the snapshot block

You'll hear it called "one vote per verified account". On the platform an account is a wallet address. A third strategy, token balance, works the same way but counts another token instead of RBNT.

Voting systems

The voting system decides how your power goes to the options. "One verified address, one vote" can also be picked as the voting system, and works as described above.

Single choice
Pick one option. All your power goes to it.
Approval
Back as many options as you like. Each gets your full power.
Weighted
Split your power across options.
Quadratic
Split your power. Large holders count for less.
Ranked choice
Rank the options. The lowest drops out each round until one has a majority.
Copeland
Rank the options. Each is compared head to head with every other.

What if I have more than one wallet?

Redbelly Access lets you add extra wallets to your verified identity, up to 10 in total. At 10, Access shows Account allocation exhausted. A wallet can belong to only one identity at a time.

The voting platform counts verified addresses, not people, and doesn't merge linked wallets. Each one verified at the snapshot votes on its own. Three linked wallets that each vote on a one-vote proposal add up to 3.00 votes. On an RBNT vote, each counts its own balance.

05/ 05 · Known limits

Known limits.Good to know before you read a result.

How the current setup behaves today, as the platform itself describes it.

identity

A verified address isn't always a different person

One identity can have up to 10 wallets, and each one counts.

timing

Late verification can't catch a live vote

Verify after a proposal opens and you can't vote on it, however long it has left.

balances

Balances freeze at the snapshot

On balance-weighted votes, tokens moved after the snapshot block change nothing for that proposal.

quorum

One big holder can meet a power quorum alone

An identity quorum only applies when the author sets one.

proposals

Live proposals can't be edited

Text, options, counting method and dates stay exactly as published.

history

Old results came from Snapshot

Proposals marked imported are past Snapshot votes. The rules on this page didn't apply to them.
